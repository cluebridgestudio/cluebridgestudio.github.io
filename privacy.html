Privacy Policy for Question Clues

Effective Date: September 30, 2026

Question Clues is designed to provide a simple and private learning experience.

Information We Collect

Question Clues does not collect personal information or personal data from users.

The app does not require users to create an account, provide a name, email address, or other personal information.

Practice Progress

Question Clues saves practice progress and practice history locally on the user's device so the app can track learning levels and display previous practice results.

This information is not transmitted to ClueBridge Studio or stored on our servers.

Users can remove individual practice sessions or reset all saved progress from within the app.

Internet and Online Services

Question Clues does not require an internet connection for normal learning and practice.

Advertising and Tracking

Question Clues does not contain advertisements and does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Question Clues does not contain in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Children's Privacy

Question Clues does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. Because the app does not collect personal information, no personal information from children is transmitted to or stored by ClueBridge Studio.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If Question Clues changes in a future version in a way that affects user privacy, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.
